## Short description
A fast, beautiful file manager for your Fire tablet. Browse storage and SD card, find every video, photo, song and document in one tap.

## Long description
OxFiles keeps everything on your Fire tablet organised.

- Categories: Images, Videos, Audio, Documents, APKs and Archives, built from a real scan of your storage and SD card
- Thumbnail grids for photos and videos
- Folder browser for internal storage and SD card
- Search across all your files
- Quick access to Downloads, Camera, Screenshots and Documents
- Dark, smooth, modern design
- No ads. No tracking. No internet permission.
