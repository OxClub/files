OxFiles Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

OxFiles is a file manager app. It does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Data access

The app accesses files on your device only to display and manage them at your request. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us or any third party.

No tracking

OxFiles contains no ads, analytics, or tracking tools.
