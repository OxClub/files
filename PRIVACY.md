# OxFiles Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

OxFiles is a file manager. It works entirely on your device.

- **We do not collect any personal data.**
- OxFiles does not request the internet permission and does not send your files, file names or any other information to us or to anyone else.
- OxFiles does not contain ads, analytics or trackers.
- The storage permission is used only to show, open and organise the files on your device and SD card.
- Files you open with another app are shared with that app only when you tap them, using Android's standard file sharing.
